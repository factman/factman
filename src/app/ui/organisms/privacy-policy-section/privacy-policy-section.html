What is collected

Nothing runs until you choose. If you accept analytics, Google Analytics 4 counts page views and a few clicks (for example, which service or project you opened) so I can see which content helps. Advertising signals are always off and Google signals are disabled.

No Google script, cookie or request is made before you accept. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, analytics stays off by default.

Cookies and storage

The contact form

The form builds an email in your own mail app. The text you type stays in your browser until you press send in that app. Analytics never receives your name, email, phone or message.
